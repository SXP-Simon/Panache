# Putting words on paper

This 72-second film, in Chinese, starts with a poem. It follows a change of pen, a fraction, a radical, and a chemical equation onto the page. A photograph of ruled paper supplies the lines for the next manuscript. At the end, you can save a single sheet as an image or export the whole document as a PDF.

## Reading parameters on a phone

Version 1.18.1 fixes clipped values on narrow screens. The font scale now shows the full 100%, and stroke widths keep their decimals and px units in view. At larger system text sizes, the label and value can move onto separate lines.

On Android, Panache now launches the system installer in a separate task so tapping Open after an update can take you back to the workbench. The first update from an older build still uses its installer flow. If Open returns you to the home screen, tap the Panache icon to continue.
