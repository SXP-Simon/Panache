# Iris and ink: a monologue in eight lines

The passage begins with two exclamations. Giving the second one, “拔吧！”, a line of its own lets the speaker pause. Two changes of thought begin the next stanzas. The final image, a feather on the speaker’s cap, has a line to itself.

## Choosing a hand

We used Zhi Mang Xing for its decisive turns and open finishing strokes. On blank paper, a type size of 41 and line spacing of 51 leave room for the strokes to breathe. A blank line separates each stanza. Fit the page in the workbench to read all eight lines at once.
