# Finding paper for a manuscript

The ruled sheet from Nanchang University and the dotted sheet from Wuhan University of Technology each have their own header and writing area. Begin with the first usable line on the paper.

Upload the background in the paper panel. Detect the ruling, then check that the writing area clears the header. For faint dotted lines, mark the area by hand. Try three short lines with punctuation and tune the top margin and line spacing before generating a whole document.

The left column shows the original backgrounds and the right shows the rendered manuscripts. Open the image to inspect the margins and baselines.
