# Type, ink and paper

**Set the page first.** Choose dimensions and margins before the type size and line spacing. If a line wraps too early, check the usable width and character spacing.

**Choose the hand.** Chinese and Latin fonts can be selected separately, and digits can use the Latin font. Keep size, position and rotation variation small for poems or equations. Use the ink controls to adjust opacity, thickness and softness.

**Save the combination.** Store a parameter preset or export its JSON to another device. The two colours in app preferences style the interface. Paper and ink have their own workbench settings.
