# Frequently asked questions

## Why do I see only the first page?

Enable all-page preview and generate again, then use the page controls. If the bottom of a single page is cropped, use Fit page.

## Why is the paper still white after I change the theme?

The two theme colours style the controls and navigation. Change paper or upload a background in the paper panel, and edit the ink separately.

## Can I work offline?

Desktop and native mobile clients include their rendering engines. The browser workbench connects to a Rust service. This project site hosts documentation and examples.

## Can I import a scanned PDF?

Text extraction depends on the PDF containing extractable text. For a page that is only a photograph, use the image as a paper background and paste the text into the editor.
